# Code of Conduct

Fouroam-V welcomes people who want to build, document, research and review software together. Everyone is expected to help keep collaboration respectful and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate of contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Discuss ideas and code respectfully, with actionable feedback.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Respect privacy, consent and applicable laws.
- Accept moderation decisions and communicate disagreements constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sexualized harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Spam, trolling, deliberate disruption or repeated bad-faith conduct.
- Encouraging piracy, credential theft or bypassing account and security protections.

## Scope and enforcement

These rules apply to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces officially maintained by the project.

Maintainers may remove content, close contributions, issue warnings or restrict participation for violations.

## Reporting

For private reports of misconduct, contact **[eu@athiliodev.com.br](mailto:eu@athiliodev.com.br)** and mention **Fouroam-V** in the subject. Do not post sensitive reports or private details in public issues. Reports will be reviewed discreetly, with access limited as appropriate.
